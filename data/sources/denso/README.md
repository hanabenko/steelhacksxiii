# DENSO Pittsburgh Innovation Lab dataset

Official source: <https://www.denso.com/us-ca/en/business/PittsburghLab/Data/>. The official description reports 17 Pittsburgh routes, 360,000 synchronized 10 Hz frames, and 36,000 3D-box-labeled frames for vehicles, pedestrians, and cyclists. It also describes GPS and vehicle-dynamics signals, making it a strong future behavioral-calibration candidate.

No public archive URL or license is published on that official page. The pipeline therefore never scrapes the site or attempts a download. Contact `support@pittsburghinnovationlab.com`, obtain permission and terms, then place a provided archive in `downloads/` and record its checksum in the manifest through normal review.
